# Privacy Policy for Hackology

*Last updated: November 2026*

Hackology is an offline puzzle game developed by Sogardev. We respect your privacy.

## 1. Information We Collect
We do not collect, transmit, or share personal or sensitive user data. Any puzzle progress or typed commands stay strictly local to your device and are never sent to external servers or third parties.

## 2. Third-Party Services
This app operates entirely offline and does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
